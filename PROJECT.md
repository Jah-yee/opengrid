# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opengrid

**Short description:** Learn grid balance, power factor correction, and frequency response (inertia and droop) in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opengrid is a beginner-friendly learning repo about how an electricity grid stays in balance. In three short sessions you go from the bar-balance picture (supply equals demand, and the 50 Hz frequency sags when demand wins), to AC power (the power triangle, power factor, capacitor correction, and why transmission uses high voltage), to a toy frequency model that you solve with your own Runge-Kutta code: the swing equation with inertia and governor droop, checked against hand formulas, plus a three-bus DC power flow and a mini-project on how much fast battery response keeps the frequency above 49.5 Hz. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified and educational only; they are never for grid operation. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX

**Repository link:** https://github.com/KrunalPanchal1995/opengrid
